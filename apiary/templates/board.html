Colony Coordination Board

The plan as a board: one card per task, coloured by who does it. The board flags any run of agent tasks that goes too long without a human checkpoint. Edits change only this page: Export plan.json saves the revised plan for create_plan, and nothing here writes the colony.

Drag onto the board

Drag a card's round handle to another card to connect them. Double-click any text to edit it. Select a card or a link and press Delete to remove it. Drag empty board to pan; scroll to zoom.
