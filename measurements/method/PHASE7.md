# Phase 7: cut the colony's overhead (pre-registered)

**This plan is committed before the first Phase 7 run. Its hypotheses, rules and exclusions do not change after data
exists.** Changes are recorded below as dated amendments.

Kevin, 2026-10-16: overhead first, then a harder benchmark (Phase 8). Cuts to try: **plan in one call** and **solo
mode**. Not chosen: a lean Forager agent, and pushing the Queen toward fewer, larger tasks.

## Why (Measured, Phase 6)

The colony matched a plain session on quality (38/38 in all nine runs) but used a median of 7.19M tokens against
0.93M (`MEASUREMENTS.md`). Broken down from the transcripts of serial 3 and parallel 3:

- Every Forager starts with about 52k to 57k tokens of context (Claude Code's system prompt, tool definitions, the
  colony's instructions), and every one of its calls re-reads it. A Forager costs 0.4M to 1.1M tokens.
- About half of a Forager's calls are protocol: loading the colony tools, `resume`, `claim_task`, `close_task`, the
  report back.
- The Queen makes one `create_task` call per task (8 or 9), each re-reading her growing context; she alone costs
  1.7M to 2.1M tokens, about twice a whole plain run.
- Parallel lanes were not clearly faster than one Forager at a time on this project.

## The two cuts

1. **Plan in one call** (spec 0.6.0, **O-5** `create_plan`): the Queen records the shared decisions and creates every
   task in one call, all or nothing, with dependencies named by slug inside the plan.
2. **Solo mode** (`castes/queen.md`): the Queen does the tasks herself, one at a time, still claiming and closing each
   through the colony (ledger, acceptance checks, guard). She dispatches Foragers only when a wave has independent
   tasks big enough to pay for a subagent's start-up, when the work would not fit in her context, or when the
   protocol needs a different agent (a Guardian verifying a Nurse's research). The audit trail stays; the subagents'
   start-up cost goes.

## Design

- **Project:** the same `bench/skirmish/SPEC.md` and the same 38 hidden tests as Phase 6, so the results compare.
- **Arms:** `solo` (the colony as it ships after this phase: apiary 0.6.x, `create_plan`, solo mode) and `plain`
  (unchanged). Three runs each, interleaved: plain 4, solo 1, plain 5, solo 2, plain 6, solo 3. New plain runs, so
  both arms see the same days and service load. Phase 6's runs are shown beside them, not pooled.
- **Same everywhere:** the machine, the model, the prompt "Build the project described in SPEC.md.", and the human
  rule (recommended answers, approve plans, no other help). Measured and graded by the same instruments as Phase 6,
  with amendments 2 and 3 applied.

## Expectations, stated before the data

My prior (Claude's): solo uses **more** tokens than plain, because of the colony instructions, the plan, the TRACEs
and the claim and close calls, but far fewer than Phase 6's colony: a median between 1.2 and 2.5 times plain's.
Quality the same. Time between plain's and Phase 6 serial's.

## Decision rules

Same rule as Phase 6: A is clearly better than B on a metric only if A's worst run is better than B's best run.

1. **Did the cuts work?** Solo against Phase 6's parallel colony (the colony as it shipped): passes if solo is clearly
   better on total tokens, with its median hidden-test score within 2 of the parallel colony's.
2. **What does the audit trail cost now?** Solo's median tokens and minutes as a multiple of plain's, from the runs of
   this phase. Reported, not passed or failed. If solo is not clearly worse than plain on tokens, the docs may say the
   colony costs no clear extra; otherwise they state the multiple.

## Exclusions

As Phase 6: only contamination or a missing transcript, and a colony run not started in its own folder (amendment 1).
An excluded run is replaced by a new run of the same arm. No run is excluded for its result.
